# GT-KPC-001 — GrowTogether Knowledge & Programme Continuity System

**Programme:** GrowTogether Enterprise Platform
**Authority:** GT Headquarters / Enterprise Programme Management Office
**Reference:** GT-KPC-001
**Classification:** Enterprise Governance / Programme Continuity
**Status:** ESTABLISHED — FOUNDATION BASELINE
**Repository Authority:** `~/Documents/GT/Enterprise-Platform/Source/growtogether-platform`

---

## 1. Purpose

GT-KPC-001 establishes the permanent GrowTogether Knowledge & Programme
Continuity System.

GrowTogether shall not depend on one chat, one AI context window, one session,
one individual, one temporary file or conversational memory for authoritative
programme continuity.

The system preserves controlled programme knowledge, decisions, engineering
evidence, capability state, recovery points and handover state in persistent,
versioned and recoverable records.

AI-generated summaries may support continuity but are not authoritative merely
because they were generated by an AI assistant.

---

## 2. Governing continuity principle

Authoritative continuity follows this precedence:

1. controlled HQ / EPMO decisions and approved programme records;
2. authoritative repository implementation and committed documentation;
3. controlled test, E2E, operational and recovery evidence;
4. controlled handover records;
5. AI-generated summaries and conversational recollection.

Where authoritative sources conflict, the discrepancy must be classified and
resolved through conformity review.

No successor workspace may silently guess, overwrite, downgrade or redesign
previously controlled work merely because contextual history is incomplete.

---

## 3. Continuity architecture

### Level 1 — Authoritative repository and governed records

Contains:

- programme decisions;
- requirements and capability definitions;
- lifecycle registers;
- architecture records;
- controlled implementation;
- tests and validation evidence;
- commit provenance;
- recovery checkpoints.

### Level 2 — Controlled handover

Contains:

- current programme state;
- verified work;
- partial work;
- approved but unimplemented work;
- reserved capabilities;
- confirmed gaps;
- not-yet-verified work;
- warnings;
- exact continuation position;
- exactly one next controlled action where applicable.

A handover does not replace Level 1 authoritative records.

### Level 3 — Backup, recovery and restoration evidence

Contains:

- backup procedures;
- recovery procedures;
- checksums where applicable;
- restoration evidence;
- recovery-test evidence;
- operational continuity evidence;
- recovery-point references.

Recovery must be demonstrated and testable rather than assumed.

---

## 4. Controlled lifecycle classifications

The following classifications are preserved across successor workspaces:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Proven with appropriate evidence. Do not downgrade without contrary evidence or authorised redesign. |
| IMPLEMENTED | Engineering exists, but verification state must still be recorded separately where applicable. |
| PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Some layers or boundaries are proven; remaining boundaries require evidence. |
| APPROVED / NOT IMPLEMENTED | Authorised requirement exists but engineering is incomplete or absent. |
| RESERVED | Governed future capability; do not force into the current release. |
| CONFIRMED GAP | Evidence establishes a missing capability or requirement. |
| NOT VERIFIED / NOT YET VERIFIED | Completion must not be claimed until evidence exists. |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced through an explicit controlled decision while historical traceability is retained. |
| ARCHIVED | Retained for history but no longer active. |

File existence alone is never proof of VERIFIED status.

## 5. Evidence chain

Where applicable, controlled capability evidence should trace:

Requirement
→ Decision / authority
→ Owning programme
→ Shared enterprise capability
→ Product implementation
→ Release
→ Backend state
→ Frontend state
→ End-to-End state
→ Repository path
→ Tests
→ Commit SHA
→ Recovery evidence
→ Current lifecycle status

Missing evidence must be recorded as missing rather than reconstructed from
assumption.

## 7. Non-duplication rule

GT-KPC-001 is an enterprise continuity layer.

It shall not:

- replace domain-specific architecture;
- duplicate operational runbooks;
- duplicate implementation documentation;
- move existing authoritative records merely for centralisation;
- create competing sources of truth.

It shall instead register, link, classify and preserve authoritative evidence
under its existing owning programme or capability.

---

## 8. Versioning and supersession

Authoritative continuity records must be version controlled.

When a decision, capability or record changes:

1. preserve the earlier history;
2. identify the superseding authority;
3. record the new lifecycle state;
4. retain provenance to prior evidence;
5. avoid silent replacement.

Destructive history rewriting is prohibited for controlled programme recovery.

---

## 9. Handover standard

A controlled handover should contain, at minimum:

- reference and revision;
- programme/workstream;
- authoritative repository;
- branch and HEAD where engineering applies;
- verified recovery commits;
- VERIFIED classifications;
- PARTIAL classifications;
- APPROVED / NOT IMPLEMENTED items;
- RESERVED items;
- CONFIRMED GAPS;
- NOT VERIFIED items;
- current operational warnings;
- unfinished work;
- exact continuation position;
- next controlled action;
- security-sensitive-information exclusion statement.

Successor workspaces must perform repository conformity before modifying
previously verified engineering.

---

## 10. AI-generated information boundary

AI assistance may:

- summarize controlled records;
- locate evidence;
- compare versions;
- identify possible duplication;
- prepare handovers;
- surface unresolved gaps;
- support programme analysis.

AI assistance must not independently:

- invent authoritative decisions;
- convert missing evidence into VERIFIED status;
- cancel approved capabilities because code is absent;
- overwrite historical programme state;
- silently resolve source conflicts;
- treat conversational memory as the source of truth.

Authoritative status comes from governed records and evidence.

---

## 11. Recovery and resilience requirements

Programme continuity shall progressively include:

- repository recovery;
- controlled document recovery;
- database and object-store recovery where applicable;
- checksums or integrity verification where appropriate;
- restoration testing;
- recovery-point recording;
- dependency mapping;
- backup evidence;
- documented RPO/RTO where operationally required.

Existing capability-specific recovery procedures remain authoritative within
their domains and shall be referenced by the continuity system.

---

## 12. Minimum permanent registers

GT-KPC-001 shall progressively establish controlled registers for:

1. programme decisions;
2. workstream / programme lifecycle state;
3. capabilities and implementation evidence;
4. recovery and handover state;
5. unresolved gaps and continuity risks.

The specialised AI capability register is governed separately as:

`GT-AI-CAPABILITY-REGISTER-001 — AI Capability Master Register`

and shall integrate with GT-KPC-001 rather than operate as an isolated
programme memory.

## 13. Current establishment state

At foundation establishment:

- existing repository documentation is preserved in place;
- existing commit evidence is preserved;
- existing operational recovery documents are preserved;
- GT Connect Section 31 recovery documentation is preserved under its existing
  controlled ecosystem record;
- no existing authoritative record has been replaced;
- GT-KPC-001 becomes the governing enterprise continuity framework.

follow-on actions.

---

## 14. Governance rule

**GrowTogether programme knowledge must survive the loss of a chat, AI session,
individual participant or temporary working context without loss of
authoritative programme state.**

The repository and governed records remain the permanent continuity foundation.
